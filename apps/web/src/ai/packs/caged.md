CAGED system (reference pack)

What it is:
- The “CAGED” system maps the same chord quality across the neck using 5 familiar open-chord shapes:
  C-shape, A-shape, G-shape, E-shape, D-shape.
- In practice, you learn *movable chord grips* (often partial grips) and how they connect.

For C major (example grips; low E -> high e, 6 chars; x=mute, 0=open):
- C-shape (open): x32010
- A-shape (barre): x35553  (full barre), or x3555x (shell/partial)
- G-shape (partial): 8 10 10 9 8 8  (write as 81010988 if using 2-digit frets; otherwise use a simpler partial like x10 10 9 8 x)
- E-shape (barre): 8 10 10 9 8 8  (same pitch set as above; common to teach E-shape at 8th fret)
- D-shape (triad/partial): x x 10 9 8 x  (top-string triad)

Note:
- For “shape practice”, partial grips (3–4 strings) are often better than full 6-string grips.
- Always connect shapes by *shared chord tones* (C–E–G) and nearest movement.

Practice ideas:
- Say the shape name out loud as you play it.
- Strum once, then arpeggiate slowly.
- Move between adjacent shapes on the same chord (C) with minimal motion.
- Apply in a simple progression: C → F → G (still triads/partials).
